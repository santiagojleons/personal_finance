How This Budget Works

Your income is divided into four buckets — every dollar gets a job. Learn what each one is, then pick a profile that fits your life.

Essentials

The non-negotiables — rent, groceries, utilities, insurance, transport. If you stopped paying these, life gets difficult fast.

Growth

Money working for future you — investing, retirement accounts (401k, Roth IRA), index funds. Also where debt payments go if you're paying off loans.

Lifestyle

Your quality-of-life spending — dining out, hobbies, subscriptions, travel, entertainment. Important to have, but the first place to trim if needed.

️ Savings

Your safety net and short-term goals — emergency fund, vacation fund, car fund, down payment. Keeps unexpected expenses from derailing your budget.
